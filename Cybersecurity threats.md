# Common Cybersecurity Threats

## 1. Phishing
Phishing is a cyber threat where criminals use fake emails, messages or websites to trick people into sharing personal information, such as passwords.

## 2. Malware
Malware is harmful software designed to disrupt devices, steal information or damage computer systems.

## 3. Social Engineering
Social engineering involves manipulating people into revealing confidential information or performing actions that may compromise their security.

## Conclusion
Understanding these threats helps people recognise suspicious activities and protect themselves online.
